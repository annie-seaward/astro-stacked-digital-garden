# Philosophy

Philosophy is the study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, values, reason, and language.

## Notes in this section

- [[Free Will]] — the question of whether we choose our choices

## Themes

Philosophy often intersects with [[Technology]] — especially as artificial minds raise new questions about consciousness, agency, and what it means to think.
