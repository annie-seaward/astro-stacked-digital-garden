# Welcome to the Garden

This is a digital garden — a place for ideas to grow and connect.

Explore topics like [[Philosophy]] and [[Technology]]. Click any link to open it as a side-by-side panel.

## What is a digital garden?

A digital garden is a personal knowledge base that emphasizes connections between ideas rather than chronological blogging. Notes link to each other, forming a web of thought.

Unlike a blog, a garden is always growing and changing. Notes are tended over time rather than published once and forgotten.

## How to navigate

- Browse topics in the sidebar
- Click any `[[wikilink]]` to open a note in a new panel beside this one
- The URL captures your current stack — you can share or bookmark any view
- Use the back button to collapse the most recent panel
