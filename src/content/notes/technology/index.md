# Technology

Technology is the application of knowledge for practical purposes. This section explores ideas at the intersection of computing, society, and mind.

## Notes in this section

- [[Language Models]] — large neural networks that model human language
- [[The Web]] — the hyperlinked document system that connected the world

## Themes

Technology raises deep philosophical questions. [[Language Models]] invite us to reconsider [[Free Will]] and agency. [[The Web]] is itself a form of digital garden — a hyperlinked knowledge space.
